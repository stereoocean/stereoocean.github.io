Assessing sea roughness from monocular imagery is challenging because open water
offers few conventional depth cues. Without stereoscopic depth perception, the
three-dimensional crests and troughs that distinguish sea states are harder to
interpret.

This output investigates the fractal nature of the sea surface and evaluates the
extent to which fractal-dimension features can inform sea-state classification from
single-view imagery.
