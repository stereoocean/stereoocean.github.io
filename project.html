Real-time semantic wave imaging for seakeeping

Enhancing Maritime Safety investigates accessible real-time wave-imaging methods for maritime operations. The project brings together stereo imaging, 4D wave reconstruction, computer vision, and experimental validation.

The research direction is to support interpretation of the wave, vessel, payload, and crew environment. This page keeps the project description high level while manuscripts are in preparation.
